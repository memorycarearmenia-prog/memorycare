## The one sentence everything serves

> This company will show me, in evidence I can check and forward to my
> family, exactly what was done at our grave and when — on a schedule I
> do not have to manage.

The verb is **show**, the object is **evidence**, the qualifier is
**checkable**. Copy that does not serve that sentence is decoration.

We are not selling grave care. We are selling an evidentiary standard
that happens to include grave care. An incumbent has sold four visits a
year with photographs in Yerevan since about 2015, so **the volume
argument does not exist** — Optimal's four visits is the count they
already advertise. What we have is *what a visit is* and *what arrives
after it*: every visit is a full visit, the whole plot and every
monument, cleaned, not a look around.

## Absolute constraints — a breach disqualifies the copy

- **No fabricated proof of any kind.** No testimonials, no review counts,
  no "X families trust us", no years in business, no customer numbers.
  The company registered in 2026 and has zero paying customers. The live
  site currently claims 150,000 customers, 250,000+ graves and 15 years
  of experience, alongside three invented testimonials illustrated with
  photographs of real public figures. That is the reason this rebrand
  exists.
- **Never** `the only` · `the first` · `nobody else` · `unlike others` ·
  `unique` · `revolutionary` · `since 20xx`. In any language.
- **No competitor is ever named or alluded to**, including inside an FAQ
  answer.
- **Never the words "light visit" or "heavy visit"**, or any translation
  of them. The owner rejected that split on 26.08 — all visits are full
  visits. They must not survive in a table column or a schema field
  either.
- **No guilt.** Never "When was the last time you visited?" or any
  variant. This audience arrives already defended against guilt; it
  closes the tab. Lead with the outcome, then name both reasons
  naturally — distance and lack of time — without addressing either
  audience as a persona.
- **No consolation register, no rhetorical questions, no "peace of
  mind".** Fact before feeling. The photographs carry the feeling so the
  words do not have to.
- **Never** put words in the dead person's mouth. No "she would have
  wanted it this way."
- **No QR code, no digital memorial page.** Year-2 scope, mentioned
  nowhere, not even as "coming later".
- **A guarantee is published only once it is legally bound.** Liability
  and worker insurance are still open. A guarantee we cannot honour is
  worse than none.

## Pricing — the owner's decision of 26.08.2026

| Product | Composition | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Զննում** (Inspection) | One orientation visit: find the cemetery and the plot, full written inventory, photo/video of the condition, a list of the work needed, a quote for minor repair. **No cleaning.** | **20,000 ֏** |
| **Էքսպրես** (Express) | One full visit: deep cleaning of the whole plot and monuments — steam cleaner, professional neutral-pH chemistry, wet/dry vacuum. **No high-pressure washing on a monument.** Photo and video reports, portal access. | **65,000 ֏** |
| **Օպտիմալ** (Optimal) — flagship | Annual: **4 full visits, one in each season** | **160,000 ֏ / year** |
| **Մաքսիմում** (Maximum) | Annual: **6 full visits** | **200,000 ֏ / year** |
| **Հատուկ խնամք** (Special) | Non-standard: more visits, a plot over 16 m², more than two monuments, several family plots. **Always begins with a Զննում.** | **calculator / consultation** |

Credits: Զննում is credited **only on signing an annual subscription**,
within 60 days, and never into an Express. Express is credited in full
into a subscription within 60 days. **One credit only** — either the
Զննում or the Express, never both; a client who bought both gets the
larger. There is **no discounted repeat Express**; it is always 65,000.

Price is flat **within a standard envelope of 16 m² and two monuments**.
Beyond it there is a published formula, the same for everyone:
+10,000 ֏/year per m² over 16, +30,000 ֏/year per monument over two
(one-off Express: +2,500 and +7,500 per visit). Sliders cap at 100 m²
and 10 monuments. Do **not** write "flat whatever the size of the plot".
Write that the formula is published and the price is visible before
anyone calls.

**The best fact on the site:** run the credit arithmetic and every route
into year one costs the same **160,000 ֏** — entering by a 20,000
inspection, by a 65,000 Express, or by buying the subscription outright.
Hence *"Starting small costs you nothing."*

Optimal sells in one sentence: **"four full visits, one in each
season."** The winter visit runs in a weather window rather than on a
date, because the protocol limit is temperature; four visits are
guaranteed regardless, and a missed winter visit is **added** to spring.
That is a contract term, not a failure, and it should be said plainly.
"Monthly" is forbidden. Optimal is marked **"Our recommendation"**, never
"most chosen" — zero customers. Armenian: `առաջատար`.

## The protocol — publish it, with the numbers

A visit cannot be closed without a GPS point taken on site, **eight
photographs (four angles before, the same four after)**, and **two videos
of 20–40 seconds**. No competitor publishes a protocol. Do not write
"detailed photo reports". Write the numbers.

Say GPS as **verification**, never as location: the point is recorded
**on site, at the plot, on the day**. It answers "was the crew standing
there", not "where is the grave".

## Languages

**Three: ARM / ENG / RUS.** French is out of scope.

**Write each language natively. Do not write English and translate it.**
Armenian that reads like a translation of an American landing page is a
failure, and it is the single most likely failure in this round.

One script per locale — no Armenian **words** in the English or Russian
versions, no product names in Armenian letters there. **This is about
words, not symbols**: ֏ (U+058F) appears in all three locales because it
is the sign for the currency the client is charged in.

Armenian and Russian run 15–30% longer than English. The character
budgets in `PROPOSAL-ux.md` §10 are the constraint, and the English
budget × 1.30 is the build target.

## The naming problem

"Memory care" in English is semantically owned by the dementia-care
industry — searching the bare brand surfaces Alzheimer's services. Never
optimise for the bare brand name. Headings and title tags are
category-first; every `h1` must contain the category and the city. There
is a **visible descriptor next to the logo** — "Grave care in Yerevan" —
and it is copy, not decoration.
