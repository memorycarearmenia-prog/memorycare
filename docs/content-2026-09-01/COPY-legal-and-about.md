## 3. Full service descriptions — `/{loc}/pricing/` and the About/services summary

Bank condition 3 asks for every tariff with its exact visit count. Five

**What a full visit is — printed once, applies to all four cleaning
products (slot 79 group):**

> A full visit covers the whole plot and every monument on it. The crew
> clears and weeds the plot, washes and cleans the monuments and the
> facing, tidies the surrounding ground, carries out seasonal care and
> removes all waste. Equipment is chosen by what the plot actually needs:
> steam generator, vacuum, professional pH-neutral chemistry, hand tools.
> Pressure washing is used on paths, kerbs and fences — never on a
> monument. Before the work, the crew records a GPS point and photographs
> four fixed angles and films a 20–40 second walk-round. After the work,
> the same four angles and the same walk-round from the same points. The
> visit cannot be closed in our system without all of it.

### 3.1 Զննում — Inspection · 20,000 ֏ AMD · one-off

**Exact visit count: 1 visit. No cleaning is performed.**

> One orientation visit. We locate the cemetery and the plot, take a GPS
> point, and make a full written inventory of everything we see: the
> monument, the facing, the plot ground, the condition of the lettering
> and any existing damage. You receive photographs and video of the
> condition as we found it, the list of work the plot needs, and a written
> quote for minor repair if any is needed. **No cleaning is done on this
> visit** beyond removing loose litter.
>
> The price is the same for a plot of any size.
>
> The 20,000 ֏ is credited **only when you sign an annual subscription**,
> within 60 days of the inspection. It is not credited into an Express.

### 3.2 Էքսպրես — Express · 65,000 ֏ AMD · one-off

**Exact visit count: 1 full visit.**

> One full visit, as defined above: deep cleaning of the whole plot and
> every monument on it. You receive the photo and video report and access
> to the client portal, where the report stays available to you.
>
> The 65,000 ֏ is credited in full into an annual subscription taken
> within 60 days, and the visit then counts as the first visit of your
> subscription year.
>
> A second Express costs 65,000 ֏, the same as the first. There is no
> reduced repeat price.

### 3.3 Օպտիմալ — Optimal · 160,000 ֏ AMD per year · Our recommendation

**Exact visit count: 4 full visits per subscription year, one in each season.**

> Four full visits, one in each season. Your subscription year runs twelve
> months from the day you sign, and the four visits are placed inside it,
> one per season.
>
> 160,000 ֏ for four visits is 40,000 ֏ a visit, and every one of them is
> the whole plot and every monument.
>
> Paid once, for the year.

### 3.4 Մաքսիմում — Maximum · 200,000 ֏ AMD per year

**Exact visit count: 6 full visits per subscription year.**

> Six full visits across the subscription year, each identical in scope to

**Never described as monthly.** Six visits is six visits.

### 3.5 Հատուկ խնամք — Special · by calculator and consultation

### 3.6 What the price covers, and what changes it

> One price list — the same in Yerevan and in Los Angeles. Prices do not

## 6. Refund and cancellation policy — `/{loc}/legal/refund/`

Bank condition 7.

**H1 (33 ch):** `Refunds and cancellation`

**Standfirst (139 ch):**
`You can cancel a subscription at any time and get back the part of the year you have not received. Here is exactly how that is calculated.`

### 6.1 The rule

> **You can cancel an annual subscription at any time.** We refund the
> visits you have paid for and not received.
>
> The refund is calculated on **the amount you actually paid**, and by
> **visits**, not by days:
>
> ```
> refund = amount you paid × (visits not performed ÷ visits in your plan)
> ```
>
> rounded **up** to the nearest 100 ֏, in your favour. **There is no
> upper limit on the refund.**

### 6.2 Why the base is the amount you paid

> If you started with an inspection or an Express visit, its price was
> credited into your subscription, so the amount you actually paid was
> less than the list price. The refund is calculated from what left your
> account, not from the list price. Calculating it the other way would
> return you more than you ever gave us, which would not make the policy
> generous — it would make it wrong.

### 6.3 Worked examples — printed on the page as arithmetic, never as a single figure

**Example 1 — Optimal, entered through an Express.**

| | |

| Amount you paid | 95,000 ֏ AMD *(160,000 less the 65,000 Express credit)* |
| Visits in your plan | 4 |
| Visits completed | 1 |
| Visits not performed | 3 |
| **Refund** | 95,000 × 3 ÷ 4 = 71,250 → **71,300 ֏ AMD** |

**Example 2 — Optimal, bought outright.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Amount you paid | 160,000 ֏ AMD |
| Visits in your plan | 4 |
| Visits completed | 2 |
| Visits not performed | 2 |
| **Refund** | 160,000 × 2 ÷ 4 = 80,000 → **80,000 ֏ AMD** |

**Example 3 — Maximum, entered through an inspection.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Amount you paid | 180,000 ֏ AMD *(200,000 less the 20,000 inspection credit)* |
| Visits in your plan | 6 |
| Visits completed | 1 |
| Visits not performed | 5 |
| **Refund** | 180,000 × 5 ÷ 6 = 150,000 → **150,000 ֏ AMD** |

### 6.4 Why visits and not days

> Because you can count visits yourself. A month-based calculation would
> tell a client who has had one visit out of four that they have used up
> 27% of the year, and there would be nothing they could check. Work
> already done is already paid for; everything else comes back.

## 10. Locale notes

- **English is the master for the privacy policy** (bank condition 6) and
  is the version a lawyer should review first.
- **Armenian and Russian are written natively, not translated.** These are
  legal documents, so the constraint is stricter, not looser: a translated
  refund policy that reads as a translation invites the reader to check it
  against the original, and §7.10 has not yet said which original wins.
- **One script per locale.** The English and Russian pages carry no
  Armenian words — the product names appear as `Inspection`, `Express`,
  `Optimal`, `Maximum`, `Special care` in English, and as their Russian
  equivalents in Russian. `֏` appears in all three, because it is the sign
  for the currency the client is charged in.
- **Budgets.** These pages are prose, so the §10 slot budgets apply only
  to the footer strings (6–12), the frozen promises (16–18) and the
  service-page slots quoted inline above. Armenian runs 15–30% longer;
  build to EN × 1.30 and check the Armenian string against the layout.
- **The three frozen strings** (callback, hours, report) must be one
  translation each, used in all six places they occur. Any local variation
  is a defect.
- **Numbers stay in Latin digits with a comma thousands separator in all
  three locales** (`160,000 ֏`), fixing FINDINGS #21.
- **The dates in the legal pages** carry the day-month-year order used in
  Armenia in all three locales, so a report date and a policy date never
  read differently.
