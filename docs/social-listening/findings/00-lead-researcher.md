## Patterns noticed

1. **The competitive claim in CLAUDE.md needs revision**: hush.am already
   combines GPS + before/after photos + flowers + a grave database +
   diaspora targeting (US phone). MemoryCare's remaining differentiators:
   video reports, client portal with family-circle accounts, subscription
   tiers, and premium brand/verification rigor.
2. **Provable unmet demand exists on Find a Grave** — open photo requests
   for Yerevan cemeteries are a direct, targetable audience.
3. **"Memory care" (EN) is semantically owned by dementia care** — plan
   SEO around compound queries, not the bare phrase.
4. The **cleaning-video genre** is a ready-made growth channel with a
   proven format (before/after + the person's story) and a known
   tone-trap (cheerfulness reads as disrespect; MemoryCare's dignity-first
   tone fits perfectly).
5. RU-language markets (Belarus, Ukraine) publish per-service price lists
   — useful for pricing-communication benchmarks.
