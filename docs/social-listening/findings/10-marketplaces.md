## Patterns (3–5)

1. **Supply everywhere is fragmented sole traders; verification is the gap.** list.am sellers file grave care under "construction" or "mourning" with no reporting promise; Profi.ru/Yandex have hundreds of bidding individuals. Only Tending (US), Grabpflegeservice24 (DE) and one Moscow firm (Ритуал Центр, 5,000 ₽ with фотоотчёт) sell proof-of-visit — and none combine photo+video+GPS+portal+family-circle. MemoryCare's differentiator holds, but photo-reports alone are already commoditized in Moscow.
2. **Two dominant price bands worldwide, and MemoryCare sits inside them.** One-off visit: ~$50–300 / 3,000–12,000 ₽ / 20,000–60,000 ֏ equivalents; annual care: ~$199–459 / 100–700 € / from 2,500 ₽×visits. MemoryCare's 180,000 ֏ (~$460) Optimal is at the top of the Tending annual range but with 6 visits vs. Tending's model — defensible if the report/portal value is shown.
3. **The funeral-home / monument-dealer upsell channel is real and organized.** Tending pays funeral homes up to $78/grave to resell it; Moscow monument makers and funeral bureaus all bolt on "уход за могилой"; DE florists e-commerce-ified it. Yerevan funeral homes and monument masons are both a competitor risk and a distribution channel.
4. **Cemetery-official care is productized abroad (Birmingham council order form, Hamburg cemeteries' online shop, US perpetual care), but no equivalent surfaced for Yerevan cemeteries** — the institutional-competitor lane appears empty in Armenia.
5. **Marketplace demand signals are strongest in Russia** — the diaspora's habit market: 159 rated providers in Moscow (avg 4.9), liquid markets even in Tula/Ulyanovsk, plus "grave care as a business" startup guides. Expect RU-style operators to copy the model into Armenia quickly; speed and the verified-portal moat matter.
