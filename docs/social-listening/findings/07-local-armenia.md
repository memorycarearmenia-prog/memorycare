## Patterns (3–5)

1. **A "4 visits + photo report" local standard already exists — but stops there.** At least three Yerevan providers (HUSH.am, Dambaran, Service Memorial) sell near-identical annual packages: 4 visits, grass+headstone cleaning, flowers, before/after photos; Dambaran adds video. None surface GPS verification, a client portal, or family sub-accounts — MemoryCare's 6/9-visit tiers and portal/family-circle stack sit clearly above the incumbent formula. Two of them (HUSH.am, Service Memorial) use near-verbatim identical package wording — the "market" may be smaller than it looks.
2. **Diaspora targeting is already explicit locally.** HUSH.am publishes a US phone number ((818) area code) and runs a grave-records database + Google Play app; Yerkramas (Armenians-of-Russia paper) publishes remote-grave-care content. The diaspora proxy-visit demand is proven, not hypothetical.
3. **Trust in official/informal cemetery caretakers is publicly broken.** Documented bribery by cemetery heads (Mushakan-Jrashen arrest, 2023, official Anti-Corruption Committee record), Hetq/IWPR corruption investigations ($700 informal payments, $7,000 illegal plot resale), and flower theft from graves. "Verified, GPS-tagged, photographed visits" answers the exact failure mode locals and diaspora complain about — this is the strongest locally-grounded trust angle for the Блок доверия section.
4. **Municipal service ends at burial.** Yerevan Municipality's published paid cemetery services cover grave digging and funeral-day logistics only (26,900–39,000 AMD range); there is no municipal aftercare product, and news coverage since at least 2011 describes overcrowding (14 of 21 cemeteries full) and underfunding. Ongoing grave care is structurally privatized-by-default into informal hands.
5. **Physical access is itself a pain point.** First-hand visitor accounts of Tokhmakh describe severe overcrowding with plots too dense to walk between and sections hard to reach — supporting both the marketing message (visiting is genuinely hard even from within Yerevan) and the operational logic of the Զննում recon visit (GPS lock-in of the plot before care visits).
