## Patterns (3–5)

1. **A local competitor layer already exists and is diaspora-facing.** HUSH.AM (annual package, 4 visits, before/after photo reports), Hangestaran (remote viewing + prayers), profibank.am (RU-language Yerevan care), plus funeral homes (Requiem International surfaced in the same searches). Photo reports are already table stakes in Armenia; none of the surfaced offers advertise GPS verification, video, a client portal, or a family-circle account — MemoryCare's differentiator holds, but "first mover" claims would be false.
2. **Competitors' own copy validates the migration/distance driver.** Hangestaran's indexed pitch literally opens with migration ("Armenians mostly have loved ones buried far away"); Tending's founder story is "a grave located far from where I live." The archive's guilt/distance persona is confirmed by the market itself, not just by our own assumptions.
3. **The Russian-language space is pre-educated on the exact model.** RU-market services (Moldova's Memory, Belarus's requiem.by, Russian restorers covered by AFP) already sell remote ordering via WhatsApp + photo proof; Russian-Armenian prospects will recognize the offer instantly — RU copy can skip explaining the concept and compete on proof quality (GPS/video/portal).
4. **Open, indexable DIRECT demand posts are scarce; the real conversation is behind walls.** No verifiable public forum/Reddit/Facebook post of an individual diaspora Armenian asking for grave care in Armenia surfaced. Demand discussion almost certainly lives in closed Facebook groups ("Armenians of…", Yerevan groups), Telegram chats, and VK — reaching it requires native accounts/community presence, not search-engine listening.
5. **Grave care is framed as identity and ritual in diaspora media, not as a chore.** US-diaspora press covers volunteer cleanups of neglected immigrant Armenian graves as acts of communal duty; France-diaspora press keeps candle-lit grave rituals emotionally present. Marketing that frames the service as continuation of duty/ritual (not outsourced cleaning) matches how these communities already talk.
