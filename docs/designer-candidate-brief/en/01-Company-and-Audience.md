# Company and audience

## Who we are

MemoryCare (Memory Care LLC, Yerevan) is a subscription service for
professional care of family memorial plots in Yerevan cemeteries:
cleaning, plant care, minor repairs, with a mandatory photo/video report
and a GPS tag for every visit, delivered through a client cabinet.

The company is registered, the memorycare.am domain is purchased, and
the platform (client cabinet, payments, reports) is being developed in
parallel with the design work.

## What problem we solve

People want to, but can't regularly care for their loved ones' memorial
plots: some live far away, others simply don't have the time. In both
cases, the result is the same — a constant sense of guilt and anxiety,
and often the person doesn't even know the current condition of the
plot.

We solve both problems at once: we care for the plot regularly, and
after every visit we show the real condition — photo, video, a point on
the map, a date.

## Two client personas — both equally important

**Persona 1 — Armenian diaspora.** Age 35–60, living in the US (Los
Angeles/Glendale, New York, Boston), France (Paris, Marseille), Russia
(Moscow), Germany, and other parts of Europe. Established, with a
family. Their parents'/grandparents' memorial plot is in Yerevan. They
visit once a year or less. Driver — the sense of duty and guilt over
distance.

**Persona 2 — a Yerevan resident.** Age 40–60, above-average income,
busy. Driver — lack of time, not distance.

**Important: one brand, one page for both personas.** Do not build
separate diaspora and local versions of the site. Do not write copy
that's only about "across the ocean" / "cheaper than flying yourself" —
that reads as excluding the local client. The headline should lead with
the **outcome** ("the memorial plot is cared for, even when you can't
be there yourself"), not the cause; the subheadline/body names both
reasons (distance OR being busy) in one natural sentence each.

## What sets us apart

There's no organized competitor in Armenia with a brand, regularity, and
reporting — only individual contractors without guarantees. Globally,
similar models are already proven (Tending in the US, Grabpflege in
Germany, Styks in Poland, GraveCareUkraine), but none of them combine
photo + video + GPS + client cabinet + the **"Family Circle"**
(relatives get their own access to all reports and can order one-off
services) — we have that from launch. More detail in
`03-Product-and-Site.md`.

## Current status

The company is at the starting line: the legal entity and bank account
are set up, field crew hiring is underway, and the first paid pilot
(5–10 visits) is planned for September 2026. There are no real clients
or testimonials yet — the site doesn't need invented reviews or
"X happy families" numbers; instead, lean on process and proof
("verified visits," "GPS-tagged reports").
