## Bank requirements for the site — they shape the architecture, not just the copy

Ameriabank (card acquiring) requires 8 specific things to be present on
the site before enabling card payments. That means, beyond the marketing
screens, the designer needs to build separate informational pages into
the site's structure:

1. **An "About us" page** — company description, legal name and
   registration details.
2. **Contact information** — phone, email, address — in the footer of
   **every** page, not just a dedicated one.
3. **A full description of services** — the pricing page with the exact
   composition of each product (see "Product and Site").
4. **Legal restrictions** — the service area, and a requirement that the
   client confirm they have the right to make decisions about the plot
   (the text comes from the lawyer, placement on the page is the
   designer's job).
5. **Real prices and currency** — prices in AMD; amounts in $/€ only as
   reference values, clearly marked as such.
6. **A privacy policy** — a separate page, mandatory in English (text
   comes from the lawyer).
7. **Return terms** — a separate page or block (text comes from the
   lawyer).
8. **Terms of service** — timing of the first visit after payment, the
   scheduling process, what happens if a visit can't happen (text comes
   from the lawyer).

The texts for items 4, 6, 7, and 8 are prepared by the lawyer — the
designer's job here is not to write them, but to make room for them in
the site's structure and present them in the same visual language, not
as "small print noise" in the footer, but as proper, restrained pages.

## Interactive element on the pricing page

A currency calculator — AMD/USD/EUR/RUB, interactive (not a static
table). Reference exchange rates are fine for now, marked as
"approximate" — a live rate feed isn't required for launch.

## Full deliverables list by stage

**Stage 1 (critical, needed by the developers first):**

- Photo/video report template — 3 layout variants (cabinet, email,
  social media) + a separate format for the "Զննում" report.
- Pricing page — 4 cards + currency calculator.
- Client cabinet screens (viewing a report, visit status, plan change,
  "Family Circle").

**Stage 2:**

- Vector logo: SVG/AI + PNG set, variants for light background, dark
  background, and monochrome.
- Compact brand book: logo with clear space, palette, typography, the
  tagline and its usage rules, tone, "do/don't" examples.
- The site's informational pages required by the bank (see above).

**Stage 3:**

- Field crew uniform — t-shirt/vest/cap, production-ready mockups.
- Vehicle wrap (Ford Transit Connect).
- Social media templates: before/after case post, testimonial post,
  seasonal post (Merelots, Zatik/Easter, Vardavar, April 24, New Year),
  Family Circle invitation.
- Business cards, letterhead, email signature.
- Site OG images and favicons.

The order between stages 2 and 3 is open for discussion with the
candidate — the only fixed point is that stage 1 can't wait.
