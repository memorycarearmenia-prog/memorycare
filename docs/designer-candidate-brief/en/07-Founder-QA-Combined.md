## Part 1 — Answers to the Visual Identity Brief

**1. What kind of feeling do you want the MemoryCare brand to communicate visually?**

Primarily trust, care, and quiet respect. Calm and professionalism
matter as much as warmth — we want people to feel reassured and taken
seriously, not comforted in a soft/sentimental way. Think "a
professional you can hand your most personal responsibility to," not "a
grief-support brand."

**2. Regarding the logo reference you shared, what do you like about it specifically?**

The symbolism and meaning, more than the execution. It reads as three
layers nested in one another: the forget-me-not (memory), the Armenian
symbol of eternity (identity/national context), and two open hands
(care). We also like the soft, linear graphic style and the light
tonality — it deliberately avoids a funeral aesthetic. We're open to a
different visual execution of that same meaning if you have a stronger
idea, as long as the three-layer concept and the light, non-somber
feeling are preserved.

**3. What kind of visual style do you have in mind for the brand?**

Light, premium minimalism: minimal, clean, elegant, warm but restrained.
"Premium" matters — the service costs a meaningful annual amount, and
the visual language needs to justify that. Deliberately not heavily
emotional or sentimental, and not cold/corporate either.

**4. Are there any specific colors, fonts, symbols, or visual elements you would like to use or avoid?**

Colors (a starting direction, not locked): olive green `#5E6A3A` (hover
`#7C8654`), anthracite `#33373C`/`#6B7075`, warm off-white `#FAFAF7`.
Open to a different palette if you can justify it.

Fonts: no typeface chosen yet — your call — but it must fully support
Armenian, Latin, and Cyrillic script in one coherent pairing.

Symbol: as above — forget-me-not, Armenian eternity symbol, two hands.

To avoid explicitly: funeral clichés (dominant black, crosses, gothic
lettering, candles), guilt-pressure messaging, excessive sentimentality,
cold corporate style, cheap stock imagery, loud discount/sale-style
elements.

**5. For the website, which specific visuals do you need?**

- A hero section built around visual proof — a sample before/after
  report (photo pair + GPS/date marker) should be visible on the first
  screen, not buried further down.
- A pricing section — 4 tiered cards, with our one-off "inspection"
  product visually set apart from the three subscription tiers.
- A dedicated report template/screen — this is our actual product, it
  needs real visual weight, not a small supporting graphic.
- Client cabinet screens — report history, visit status, plan
  management, and our "Family Circle" feature (a subscriber can invite
  relatives, who get their own access to reports).
- Icons/illustrations for a simple "how it works" flow (subscribe →
  visits → photo/video/GPS report).
- Trust-building visuals (verification, regularity, transparency) aimed
  at a general audience, not narrowly diaspora-coded.

**6. Do you also need social media and advertising visuals?**

Yes. Platforms: Instagram and Facebook primarily (that's where our
diaspora audience is), plus creative for Google Search ads. Types
needed: before/after case posts, testimonial posts, seasonal posts (we
have five recurring occasions on the Armenian calendar), and a "Family
Circle" invitation post. We don't have a fixed number of designs decided
yet — happy to scope that together once we agree on templates.

**7. Are there any brands, websites, or visual references you like?**

tending.app (closest direct competitor by category, calm tone),
headspace.com (calm without funeral overtones), stripe.com (clean,
trustworthy, especially around payment), airbnb.com (card-based,
photo-first layout approach).

**8. How would you like the brand to look compared to competitors?**

Compared to local competitors in Armenia (unbranded individual
contractors with no reporting or guarantees), we want to look organized,
professional, and trustworthy — the first real brand in this category
here. Compared to the handful of similar services abroad (US, Germany,
Poland, Ukraine), we want to look more complete and more premium — none
of them combine photo + video + GPS + a client cabinet + family access
the way we do, and the visual identity should make that completeness
feel obvious rather than needing to be explained.

**9. What is your deadline for the first set of designs, and when do you need the full project completed?**

Our development team is already mid-way through its build window, so the
report template, pricing page, and client-cabinet screens are the
priority and are needed as soon as possible — realistically within the
first couple of weeks. Full project completion has previously been
targeted at around one month total. *(Exact calendar dates to be
confirmed by the founders — not yet fixed as of this writing.)*

**10. What is your budget range for this project?**

Our reference point from a previous scope (logo, brand book, report
template, site mockups, uniform, vehicle wrap, and social templates) was
1,000,000 AMD. That's a starting reference, not a fixed ceiling — open
to discussing scope and price together based on what you propose.

## Part 2 — Original Design Brief, Translated (06.08.2026, for the previous designer)

**COMPANY NAME:** MemoryCare (Memory Care LLC)
**DATE:** 06.08.2026
**CONTACT PEOPLE / ROLE:** Hayk Manukyan — CBDO · David Hambardzumyan — CEO
**PHONE:** Hayk +374 93 154108 · David +374 55 315323
**EMAIL:** memorycarearmenia@gmail.com (info@memorycare.am — coming soon)

### 1. Company and mission

**01. About the company and its history.** MemoryCare is a Yerevan-based
company, founded in 2026. The idea was born from a simple observation:
hundreds of thousands of Armenians live far from their family memorial
plots and carry a quiet sense of guilt, while those living in Yerevan
don't always have the time either. We are the first professional
subscription service in Armenia for the care of family memorial plots,
with a photo/video report for every visit. The company is registered,
the memorycare.am domain is purchased, the platform is in development;
launch follows once the website is ready.

**02. The project's core mission.** To give people peace of mind — that
their family's memorial plots are cared for and surrounded by care, even
when they can't be there themselves. We sell not just cleaning, but
confirmed care: every visit is proven with photo, video, and GPS.

**03. Priority services.** Annual subscriptions: "Optimal Care" (6
visits, 180,000 AMD), "Maximum Care" (12 visits, 240,000 AMD), "Express
Care" (1 visit, 60,000 AMD). In our view, Optimal will be the
best-selling package. The "Express Care" amount is fully credited toward
the annual subscription if purchased within 60 days. If the customer
doesn't move to an annual subscription within 60 days of ordering
"Express," a repeat use of the "Express" package within that same year
is priced at 40,000 AMD. An important platform feature is the "Family
Circle": the customer invites relatives, who can see the reports and
order one-off services.

> *Note (14.08.2026): superseded by the current lineup — see
> `03-Product-and-Site.md`. There are now 4 tiers (an "Inspection" tier,
> "Զննում," was added), and "Maximum" is 9 visits/year, not 12.*

**04. Competitive advantage.** Care for family memorial plots in Armenia
is an informal market — no brand, no regularity, no reporting. We are
the first organized brand: subscription + photo + video + GPS + personal
page + "Family Circle." This complete package isn't matched even by
global analogs (Tending in the US, Grabpflege in Germany).

**05. Why people choose us.** Because we remove the main fear — "I'll
pay, but will they actually do it?" Every visit is confirmed with
before/after photos, video, and GPS on the personal page. Plus: a
professional team and equipment, clear pricing with no hidden terms,
respect for the family memorial plot.

### 2. Target audience

**06. Our core customer.** Two portraits. (1) Primary: Armenian
diaspora, 35–60 years old, living in the US/France/Russia/Europe,
established, with a family; their parents' or grandparents' family
memorial plot is in Yerevan; they visit once a year or less often;
driver — a sense of duty and guilt over distance. (2) A Yerevan resident,
40–60 years old, above-average income, busy; driver — lack of time and a
desire for predictable quality.

**07. Geography.** US (Los Angeles/Glendale, New York, Boston), France
(Paris, Marseille), Russia (Moscow), Germany and other European
countries + Yerevan.

**08. What problem are we solving?** People want to, but can't regularly
care for their family memorial plots — some are far away, some don't
have time. The result is a feeling of guilt and anxiety, and many don't
even know what condition their relative's memorial plot is in right now.
We solve both: we care for it regularly, and after every visit we show
the actual condition.

### 3. Brand identity

**09. What feeling should the site evoke?** Calm, trust, and solidity:
"these are professionals you can trust with the most personal thing."
Bright, warm, dignified, solid. Not mourning and not sadness — care and
attentiveness. The person should breathe out, not tense up.

**10. The brand as a person (3–5 words).** Reliable · caring · reserved
· professional · respectful. The character — a grown child in the
family, entrusted with what matters most.

**11. Core values.** Four simple values: (1) proven care — we show what
we did, not just promise it; (2) regularity — care is periodic, not a
one-off; (3) honesty — clear prices, clear terms; (4) respect — for both
the memory and the family. Tagline: "Honoring Memory, Caring for Loved
Ones."

**12. Mandatory visual features.** We have logo references (see section
4), but regardless of them, the designer may also propose a completely
different solution. There are no other mandatory requirements.

### 4. Logo and visual style

**13. What do we like about the current logo?** The symbolism and
meaning: three layers — forget-me-not (memory), Armenian symbol of
eternity (identity), two hands (care). The soft linear graphics, the
bright tonality, not a funeral aesthetic.

**14. What would we like to improve?** Right now we have reference
sketches. We need: clean vector execution, balanced detail (legibility
at small sizes — favicon, uniform badge), variants for light/dark
backgrounds and monochrome. We allow simplifying details for
scalability.

**15. Are we ready for a full transformation?** Yes, if the designer
justifies that it's necessary or substantially beneficial for the
business. We're open to new ideas.

**16. Mandatory elements to preserve.** The designer is free in
everything. The only preserved and non-negotiable point is the name —
MemoryCare.

**17. Mandatory colors/fonts.** The current palette is preferred — olive
green #5E6A3A (hover #7C8654), anthracite #33373C/#6B7075, warm white
#FAFAF7 — but not mandatory: if the designer proposes a better option
for the business, that's acceptable. Key criterion: easy readability.
Fonts — designer's choice; requirement: full support for Armenian,
Latin, and Cyrillic.

### 5. Website goals and design

**18. What's unsatisfying about the current site?** The current site is
a temporary landing page: empty placeholders instead of real photos and
contacts, no personal page and no payment, no before/after cases, the
visual doesn't reach the price's premium level, weak mobile version.

**19. Pages requiring the most attention.** (1) Home — trust within 5
seconds; (2) pricing — with a currency calculator and solid emphasis on
the leading package; (3) report page/screen — what the thing they're
paying for actually looks like (this is the product); (4) "How it
works"; (5) personal page and "Family Circle."

**20. New sections/features.** Personal page with photo/video/GPS
reports · online payment with international cards · "Family Circle"
(invite relatives, order one-off services) · before/after case gallery ·
"Express Care" credit mechanic · 3 languages (ARM/ENG/RUS).

**21. What can be removed/simplified?** We'll discuss this with the
designer during the work, based on their suggestions.

**22. The site's main goal.** Lead generation. Since the subscription
amount isn't small and the service is annual, it's right to start with a
free consultation request and contact; then sales. Online payment
remains available for customers who are ready.

**23. Overall style.** Light premium minimalism: an organic bright
theme, lots of air, large typography, restrained elegance (editorial),
real photos instead of stock, gentle animations. Premium, but warm.

**24. References.** tending.app (a direct analog — calm tone, category)
· headspace.com (calm without mourning) · stripe.com (cleanliness and
trust around payments) · airbnb.com (card-based, photo-first approach).

**25. What to avoid.** Funeral clichés (dominant black, crosses, gothic
lettering, candles), guilt-pressure, excessive sentimentality, cold
corporate style, cheap stock, noisy discount elements.

### 6. Content and materials

**26. The texts.** Will be updated for the new design; the foundation is
ready and we'll provide it. If necessary, we're also ready to change the
texts entirely to match a new structure.

**27. New photo/media.** Yes — a professional shoot is planned (drone +
camera, budget allocated): the team in uniform, the work process,
before/after cases from the September pilot.

**28. Do we currently have professional photos?** No — until the shoot,
the designer works with clean, temporary placeholders.

**29. What materials will we provide?** Everything we have: reference
photos of the logo symbols, the color palette with hex codes, the
tagline and its translations, the site texts in 3 languages, the
tariffs and service descriptions, the platform's functional requirements
(for coordination with the developers). There's no brand book yet — the
designer creates it themselves (as part of the contract).

### 7. Interaction and success criteria

**30. Main call to action (CTA).** Since the subscription amount is
significant and the service is annual, the primary CTA is a free
consultation request and contact with us (name + phone/WhatsApp), not a
direct purchase. Secondary CTA: "Choose a package" for customers who are
already ready, with online payment.

**31. The first 5 seconds.** Understand: "professional care for family
memorial plots in Armenia, with photo/video proof of every visit." Feel:
calm and trust. Visual solution: a before/after report example right on
the first screen.

**32. Measurable success KPIs.** Visitor→lead conversion ≥3% ·
lead→payment conversion ≥40% · 90+ paying customers within 16 weeks of
launch · customer acquisition cost (CAC) ≤ 25,000 AMD · mobile load time
<3 sec · mobile conversion ≥ desktop.

**33. Timelines and constraints.** Timeline — ~1 month, per contract.
Important: developers are building the platform in parallel (~45 days);
mockups of the key screens are needed by them sooner than the full brand
book. Mobile-first is mandatory (the diaspora comes in from their
phones). Fonts must support Armenian/Latin/Cyrillic. Delivery of source
files (Figma/AI) is a condition of acceptance.
