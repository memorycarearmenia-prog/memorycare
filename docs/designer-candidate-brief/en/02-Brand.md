# Brand

## What's fixed (the one hard constraint)

The name — **MemoryCare**, one word, mixed case ("Memory" + "Care").
Legal entity: Memory Care LLC. This is the **only** thing that's not up
for discussion. Everything else on this page is direction and
reference, not a spec: if you have a better solution and can justify it
to the business — propose it, we're open.

## Tagline

**"Honoring Memory, Caring for Loved Ones."**

Localized ARM/RUS versions are not yet finalized — that can be
discussed as part of the brand book work.

## Symbol (reference, not dogma)

See the files in `assets/brand/`:

- `forget-me-not-reference.jpeg` — the Armenian forget-me-not
  (Անմոռուկ): 5 rounded petals, fine radiating stamens at the center.
- `eternity-symbol-reference.jpeg` — the Armenian symbol of eternity
  (Հավերժություն / aravakhach): an 8-blade "pinwheel" with a
  scalloped/wavy outer edge and a small circular opening at the center.
  **This is not rings** — if you see the symbol described anywhere as
  "rings" or "interlocking rings," that's an error, not the reference.
- `logo-reference.jpeg` — both elements together, a reference sketch:
  the forget-me-not sits on top of the eternity symbol, which in turn
  sits between two open hands.

We're open to a full rebrand of the symbol if you can justify why it's
better for the business.

## Why these specific elements

The meaning we like in the reference sketch, and want preserved through
any redesign (even if you propose a different form): the logo reads as
three layers nested inside one another. The forget-me-not — memory. The
Armenian eternity symbol — identity, national context. The two hands —
care. Soft linear graphics, a light tonality — deliberately not a
funeral aesthetic.

Practical requirement for the vector redraw: the symbol must stay
legible at small sizes — favicon, a badge on the crew uniform.
Simplifying detail for that purpose is fine, as long as the three
layers stay recognizable.

## Palette (desired, not mandatory)

- Olive green `#5E6A3A` (hover `#7C8654`) — primary accent.
- Anthracite `#33373C` / `#6B7075` — primary text, dark surfaces.
- Warm white (not pure `#FFFFFF`) `#FAFAF7` — page background.

The criterion matters more than the exact hex values — easy readability
and a premium feel without coldness. If you propose a different palette
with justification, we'll consider it.

## Typography

Full support for Armenian, Latin, and Cyrillic in one font pairing (or a
coordinated family) is required. The choice of specific typefaces is up
to the designer.

## Tone and mood

Light, premium minimalism: lots of white space, large typography,
restrained "editorial" elegance. Warm, but professional — the person
should exhale, not tense up.

**Brand as a person (3–5 words):** reliable, caring, reserved,
professional, respectful. The image is an adult child in the family, the
one trusted with what matters most.

**References we like:** tending.app (a direct category analog, calm
tone), headspace.com (calm without funeral overtones), stripe.com
(cleanliness and trust around payments), airbnb.com (card-based,
photo-first approach).

**What to clearly avoid:**

- Funeral clichés: dominant black, crosses, gothic type, candles.
- Guilt-tripping the reader.
- Excessive sentimentality.
- Cold corporate style.
- Cheap stock imagery instead of real material.
- Loud discount elements.
