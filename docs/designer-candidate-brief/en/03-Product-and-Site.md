# Product and site

## Pricing lineup — 4 products, one price for every client

No surcharge for plot size, and no difference between local clients and
diaspora clients.

| Tier (client-facing Armenian name) | Composition | Price | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Զննում** (Inspection) | 1 visit: photo/video of the plot's current condition + a priced list of recommended work. No cleaning is performed | 20,000 AMD | A one-off service, not a subscription — should visually stand apart from the three annual packages on the pricing page. Low barrier to entry + "see what kind of company we are" |
| **Էքսպրես խնամք** (Express) | 1 full visit | 60,000 AMD | One-off service / trial |
| **Օպտիմալ խնամք** (Optimal) — flagship | 6 visits per year | 180,000 AMD | Expected primary choice — mark it as the leading option (not literally "bestseller," something more understated) |
| **Մաքսիմում խնամք** (Maximum) | 9 visits per year | 240,000 AMD | Premium tier. **Important: never describe it as "monthly"** — we promise a specific number of visits, not calendar regularity |

Prices are in Armenian dram (AMD). If you show amounts in $/€, always
mark them clearly as approximate/reference values — the actual charge is
in AMD.

## "Family Circle" — key differentiator, give it visibility

The subscription owner invites relatives via a link. An invited relative
gets their own access — they can see all reports and order one-off
services (for example, flowers for a memorial date with a separate
photo report). This works from platform launch, not deferred to later.
On the site, this isn't a minor feature but a full, dedicated block —
it's something no global analog offers in this form.

## The report is the actual product

The client isn't paying for cleaning (they can't verify it in person),
but for proof that the care happened. That's why the report template is
the designer's priority task (see `00-Overview.md`).

What goes into a report per the crew's field protocol:

- 4 fixed angles **before** work + the same 4 angles **after**, from the
  same points (overall view of the plot, monument front-on, close-up of
  the inscription/portrait, surrounding area).
- A video walkthrough of the plot, 20–40 seconds, before and after.
- GPS tag and date/time of the visit.
- A short comment from the crew about the work performed.

Formatting requirements: photos shouldn't be overloaded with graphics —
no frames, bright badges, or captions over the image (the person is
looking at their parents' resting place; visual noise is out of place).
A date/GPS mark is needed, but it should be understated. Three layout
variants are needed: for the client cabinet, for email, for social
media. A separate, different format is needed for the "Զննում" report
(no before/after pair there — just current condition + a priced list of
recommended work).

## Site sections

1. **Home screen** — the offer + visual proof (an example before/after
   report) right on the first screen.
2. **Pricing** — 4 cards, "Զննում" visually set apart.
3. **What proof looks like** — a dedicated screen with a sample report;
   this is the company's real product, give it real visual weight, not
   a small block.
4. **How it works** — subscribe → visits → photo/video/GPS report.
5. **Family Circle** — a dedicated block.
6. **Trust block** — verification, regularity, transparency, for both
   audiences (not diaspora-only trust signals).
7. **Language switcher** — 3 languages: Armenian, English, Russian.
8. **Clear call to action.**

## Main call to action

**A request for a free consultation** (name + phone/WhatsApp) — not a
direct purchase. The annual amount is significant, and the decision
gets made after a conversation. Online payment is the second most
important action, for clients who are already ready.

## Technical constraints for the mockups

- **Mobile-first is mandatory** — the diaspora audience mostly arrives
  from a phone.
- 3 languages: Armenian, English, Russian (a switcher, full translation,
  not decoration).
- The font pairing must fully support all three languages.
- The platform (cabinet, payment, reports) is being built in parallel
  with the design — the cabinet mockups and the report template are
  needed by the developers earlier than the full brand book.
