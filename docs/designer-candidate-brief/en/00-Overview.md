# MemoryCare — briefing package for designer candidates

This package is what to send a candidate for the designer role (logo,
brand book, website/platform) to get acquainted with the project before
starting work, or before they give an estimate/proposal. Compiled
14.08.2026 from the company's internal business archive.

## What's inside

1. `01-Company-and-Audience.md` — who we are, what problem we solve,
   for whom.
2. `02-Brand.md` — the name (the only unchangeable element), the
   tagline, symbol references, palette, typography requirements, tone.
3. `03-Product-and-Site.md` — pricing tiers, the key product mechanic
   (the "Family Circle"), what to show on the site, languages.
4. `04-References-and-Success-Criteria.md` — what to aim for, what to
   avoid, how we'll know the design worked.
5. `05-Materials-and-Full-Deliverables-List.md` — what the company
   provides upfront (existing site copy, legal details), the bank's
   requirements for the site's structure (8 mandatory pages), the full
   deliverables list by stage.
6. `06-Current-State.md` — concrete critique of the current temporary
   site (what must be fixed), the live site as a reference with a
   caveat, additional meaning behind the symbol.

Plus reference images of the symbol and logo — `assets/brand/` in the
repository root:

- `forget-me-not-reference.jpeg` — forget-me-not (component 1)
- `eternity-symbol-reference.jpeg` — eternity symbol (component 2)
- `logo-reference.jpeg` — both components together, as a reference sketch

## What we need from the designer first

The order matters — part of the work runs in parallel with platform
development (~45 days for the developers), and without mockups the
developers are blocked:

1. **Photo/video report template** — this is the company's actual
   product (see 03). Needed before anything else.
2. **Pricing page** — 4 cards, with "Զննում" visually set apart from
   the three annual packages.
3. **Client cabinet screens** — viewing a report, visit status, plan
   change, "Family Circle".
4. After that — informational pages required by the bank, the full
   brand book, vector logo, crew uniform, vehicle wrap, social
   templates, business cards. Full list in `05-Materials-and-Full-
   Deliverables-List.md`.

## Format and timeline (reference from the previous contract — negotiable)

- Timeline — about 1 month.
- Previous contract's budget — 1,000,000 AMD for the full package (logo
  + brand book + report template + site mockups + uniform + vehicle
  wrap + social templates). Not a hard reference point — open for
  discussion with the candidate.
- Source files (Figma/AI) are handed over to the company as a condition
  of each stage's acceptance.
- Mobile-first is mandatory — most traffic comes from phones.
