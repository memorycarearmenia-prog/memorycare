# Survey lead-gen copy (EN) — personal network outreach

Purpose: warm, personal messages sent by Hayk and/or David to their own
contacts (LinkedIn connections, friends, family) to drive completions of the
discovery survey. Not a mass-marketing blast — meant to be sent (or lightly
personalized and pasted) one-to-one or in small batches to people you
actually know.

Base survey link: https://questionnairememorycare.netlify.app/

## Links with source tracking

Use the tagged link for the channel you're actually sending through, so
completions can be split by channel later:

- Email: `https://questionnairememorycare.netlify.app/?src=email`
- LinkedIn DM: `https://questionnairememorycare.netlify.app/?src=linkedin`

(If the survey tool doesn't currently read/store the `src` query param,
flag that to whoever owns the survey site — the link works either way, it
just won't be attributable until that's wired up.)

---

## Which version to use

- **Email** (below) — for contacts where you'd naturally write an email, or
  where you want a bit more warmth/context up front.
- **LinkedIn DM** (further down) — for the primary channel. Short on
  purpose; LinkedIn messages get skimmed in seconds, not read like email.

Optional: if the contact clearly has a personal tie to Armenia (family
buried there, grew up there, etc.), lead with the opening line under
"For contacts with a personal tie to Armenia" instead of the default —
same email, just a more direct first line.

---

## Email version

### Subject line (pick one)

- A small favor — 3 minutes of your time?
- Quick favor before we launch something in Armenia
- Building something for families like ours — need your take

### Email body

Hi [Name],

Hope you're doing well — it's been a while!

I wanted to reach out about something I've been working on with a close
friend, David. We're building **MemoryCare** — a service in Armenia that
takes care of family graves: regular cleaning, upkeep, small repairs, and a
verified photo report after every visit, so you always know your loved one's
resting place is looked after — even from thousands of miles away.

If you're anything like me, this hits close to home. Living far from Armenia
means you can't visit as often as you'd like, and that gap weighs on you.
That's exactly the problem we're trying to solve.

Before we go further, we want to build this around what people actually
need — not guesses. So I put together a short survey (3-4 minutes, no
sign-up) to hear your honest thoughts:

**👉 [https://questionnairememorycare.netlify.app/?src=email](https://questionnairememorycare.netlify.app/?src=email)**

Your answers — even a quick "not relevant to me, but here's what I think" —
would genuinely help us get this right.

And if you know anyone else who might have a view on this (diaspora family,
friends back in Armenia, anyone), I'd be really grateful if you forwarded
this along. The more perspectives we hear, the better we can build it.

Thank you so much — it means a lot to have people like you in my corner.

Warmly,
[Your name]

P.S. No pressure at all if it's not for you right now — even just passing it
on to someone it might resonate with would be a huge help.

### For contacts with a personal tie to Armenia

Swap the second paragraph above for this more direct version, if you know
the person still has family buried in Armenia or a real connection to it:

> I know you still have family back in Armenia, and I imagine the distance
> makes it hard to look after things the way you'd want to. That's exactly
> the problem we're trying to solve.

## Notes for whoever sends this

- Replace `[Name]` and `[Your name]` — this is meant to read as a real,
  personal note, not a template. Swap in a shared memory/inside reference
  with that contact if you have one; it will land better than the generic
  version.
- Keep the forward-to-a-friend ask soft ("if you know anyone") — don't turn
  it into a two-part CTA that feels like a chain letter.
- Do not attach pricing, testimonials, or "X families trust us" — the survey
  itself should be the place that gathers that context, and the company is
  still pre-launch (no live customers yet, per project ground rules).
- If David is sending this to his own contacts, he can use the same text
  under his own name — it's written to work for either sender without
  implying a specific relationship to the recipient.
- Use the `?src=email` / `?src=linkedin` links so completions can later be
  split by channel — don't send the untagged base link.
- Keep the tone personal even with tracking added: this is a `src` query
  param on the link, not open/click tracking pixels or timers in the
  message itself. Don't add more instrumentation than that — the brand's
  whole positioning is "quiet trust," not growth-hacking.
