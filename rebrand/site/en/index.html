How it works, in full

What a full visit includes

The crew

Your plot is assigned to one crew, and they work the whole plot, not a part of it.

The equipment

Steam generator, industrial wet-and-dry vacuum, soft brushes and hand tools. High pressure for paths and railings, never for stone.

The products

pH-neutral products only, chosen for the stone: granite, lalvar, basalt or tuf. No chlorine and no acid, on any of them.

The record

A visit cannot be closed until the GPS point, eight photographs and two videos exist.

What we do not do

  • No repairs or construction without the municipality’s permission.
  • Nothing on a neighbouring plot, and no other grave in a photograph.
  • No painting and no moving part of a monument without agreeing it first.

Questions people ask first

I am not sure exactly where the plot is. Can you still help?

Yes. The cemetery, the district or just the city is enough, and “not sure” is a valid answer on our form. Finding the plot is the first thing an inspection does: we locate it, record its GPS point, photograph and film its condition, and send you a written inventory with a price for the work it needs.

What happens in winter?

The winter visit runs on the days the weather allows rather than on a fixed date, because the limit is temperature: washing stone at or below +4…+10 °C, or with a frost due within 48 hours, damages it. If no such day comes all winter, the winter visit is added to spring. Four full visits either way. That is a term of the contract.

What happens if the crew cannot reach the plot?

We tell you the same day, with the crew’s account of why — a funeral, a closed section, a dispute about access. The visit is not lost and it does not come out of your year. We go back, and you have the new date before you have to ask for it.

Can the rest of my family see the reports without paying?

Yes. Family Circle is part of every annual subscription. You invite relatives by email or WhatsApp, and they see every report from the first one, with nothing to pay and no subscription of their own. Prices, invoices and the renewal date stay visible to you alone. A report can also be forwarded as a plain link that needs no account.

Can someone from the family be there when the crew comes?

Yes. Tell us in advance and we agree a time. A relative in Yerevan can meet the crew instead of you, without an account and without an invitation, and they never see a price. Leave their name and number in the note field on the form.

What happens after I request a consultation?

We call or write within one business day, Yerevan business hours, 09:00–18:00 (UTC+4). We write on WhatsApp from +374 41 188 800 first and call only if you would rather talk. The conversation is about your plot and what it needs. Nothing is sold in it, and it costs nothing to have it.
