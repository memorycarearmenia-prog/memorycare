What we do not and cannot do

Some of these are limits we set ourselves, and some are limits the law and the cemetery set for us. It is easier to read them before you order.

What we are not

We are not a funeral service, a monument workshop, a cemetery administration or a burial-records office. We do not sell plots and we do not arrange burials.

In English, “memory care” usually means care for people living with dementia. That is not what this company does. We look after graves.

Construction and anything structural

Cleaning and care need nobody’s permission but yours. Construction work on a plot does. Building, installing, replacing, re-setting or removing any structural element of a grave requires permission from the municipality, and we do not carry out that kind of work under a care subscription.

What we do under a subscription is care: cleaning, washing, weeding, clearing, levelling loose ground, seasonal work and waste removal.

An inspection may include a written quote for minor repair. That quote is an offer for a separate, itemised job — never part of a subscription, never started without your written approval, and never started where a municipal permission is required until that permission exists.

The boundary is the stone. We do the work that does not touch a monument: straightening a fence, relaying a slab, topping up soil, painting metal. Anything on the monument itself — a chip, a crack, lettering, moving it — is a stonemason's work. We do not do it, we do not quote it, and we do not subcontract it inside a subscription.

What we will not do without asking you first

  • We do not move, lift or re-set any part of a monument.
  • We do not paint, coat or re-letter a monument.
  • We do not fell trees or work on a neighbouring plot.
  • We do not use chlorine or acid on any stone, ever.
  • We do not pressure-wash a monument.
  • We do not steam a monument that does not need it.

Yerevan’s stone is granite, lalvar, basalt and tuf. Chlorine and acid destroy all four from inside: the salts crystallise in the pores and split the surface. Over-cleaning is damage, and on tuf it cannot be undone.

On whose authority we work

There are no cemetery operators in Armenia; the municipality performs that function. We work on a plot on the authority of the person who holds the right to it, and that permission is a separate, explicit confirmation you give when you order — not a line buried in a contract.

If you are not the holder of that right — you are a grandchild, or the plot stands in the name of the person buried in it — tell us before you order. We will say what we can do.

Photography

Photographs and video of your plot are the product, and they are made on every visit. Publishing any of them anywhere outside your own report and your own Family Circle requires your separate written consent, given case by case. We do not deliberately photograph other people’s graves or other visitors.

Limits on what we can promise

If our crew damages anything at the plot, we restore it to its former condition at our cost, and there is no ceiling on that. We answer for damage we cause. We do not answer for the condition we recorded and photographed before starting, for damage caused by weather, by the cemetery, or by anyone else, or for work on the monument itself, which we do not carry out.
