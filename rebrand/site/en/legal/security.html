Security and card data

The connection between your browser and this site is encrypted, and we hold the certificate that proves it. Anything you type into the form travels encrypted.

We do not store card numbers. When card payment is enabled, the card details are entered on the bank’s own payment page, not on ours. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, the amount and the date — never the number.

We will never ask you for your bank account number, and we may not use it for anything other than accepting a payment. If anyone asks you for it in our name, it is not us.

A card payment is never accepted against a debt incurred earlier. You pay for a service before it is delivered, or you do not pay at all.

What we do store is your name, your phone number and email address, the plot we look after, the reports, and the record of what you paid. That list, and who else can see it, is in the privacy policy.

Payment

Card numbers never reach us. A card payment is entered on the bank’s own payment page and we never see, hold or store the number.

Transactions made inside Armenia are made in Armenian drams. All our prices are quoted in drams and every charge is made in drams.

Transactions performed inside Armenia are made in Armenian drams only.

There is no free trial and no preferential trial period for this service. Nothing renews by itself and nothing is charged automatically.
