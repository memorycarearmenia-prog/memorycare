How the service works

A visit to a named family plot in a Yerevan cemetery, carried out to one protocol, with a report delivered afterwards. Either as a single visit, or as an annual subscription of four or six visits. Subscriptions are paid once, for the year. There is no monthly or instalment option.

Every visit is the same full visit. The only difference is how many.

  • Inspection — 20,000 ֏ AMD
  • Single visit — 65,000 ֏ AMD
  • Four visits a year — 160,000 ֏ AMD
  • Six visits a year — 200,000 ֏ AMD
  • By arrangement

Prices 1–4 cover a plot of up to 16 m² with up to two monuments.

For a plot over 16 m², more than two monuments, more than six visits a year, or several family plots.

It begins with an inspection: we price the work after seeing the plot, not before.

Where the service is performed, and what is delivered to you

The care itself is performed at the plot, in a cemetery in Yerevan, Armenia. That is the place of delivery and it does not change with the country you are paying from. We work on Yerevan cemeteries only.

What is delivered to you is the visit report — eight photographs, two videos, one GPS point, the crew’s note — delivered electronically and reachable from any country. There is no physical delivery and nothing is posted to you.

One price list — the same in Yerevan and in Los Angeles.

From your request to your first visit

  1. You send a consultation request. No payment, no account.
  2. We call or write within one business day, Yerevan business hours, 09:00–18:00 (UTC+4).
  3. We agree the plot and the plan, and you confirm in writing that you hold the right to have work done on it.
  4. You pay, by bank transfer today, and by card once card acceptance is enabled.
  5. We locate the plot and record its coordinates, and your account opens.
  6. [the number of days between payment and the first visit is being set by the owner and will be printed here as a number]
  7. Your report arrives within 48 hours of the visit.

What arrives after every visit

A GPS point recorded by the crew standing at your plot, on the day. Eight photographs: four fixed angles before the work and the same four from the same points after it. Two videos of 20 to 40 seconds, before and after. The crew’s written note. Your visit cannot be closed in our system without every one of those.

If you asked for it, the report also arrives as a plain link you can forward to family who have no account. If you asked for it, we tell you the day before a visit — and we can tell someone else instead, such as a relative in Yerevan.

The subscription year

Your subscription year runs twelve months from the day you sign. Seasons are a promise inside those twelve months: one visit in each of them. Renewal is offered against your own anniversary date, not a company calendar. If you started with a single visit and it was credited into the subscription, that visit counts as the first visit of your year.

Paid once, for the year.

Winter

Stone cannot be washed in the cold. Our protocol forbids deep cleaning at +4…+10 °C or below and when frost is expected within 48 hours, so the winter visit is scheduled on the days the weather allows rather than on a fixed date. If no suitable day comes at all, that visit is added to the spring one, and you still receive four visits. This is a term of your contract.

The crew

Your plot is looked after by the same assigned crew.
