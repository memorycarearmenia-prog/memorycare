Refunds and cancellation

You can cancel a subscription at any time and get back the part of the year you have not received. Here is exactly how that is calculated.

The rule

You can cancel an annual subscription at any time. We refund the visits you have paid for and not received. Cancellation takes effect immediately and the refund is automatic.

refund = the amount you paid × visits not performed ÷ visits in your plan

Rounded up to the nearest 100 ֏, in your favour. There is no upper limit on the refund.

Why visits and not days

Because you can count visits yourself. A month-based calculation would tell a client who has had one visit out of four that they have used up 27% of the year, and there would be nothing they could check. Work already done is already paid for; everything else comes back.

Why the base is the amount you paid

If you started with an inspection or a single visit, its price was credited into your subscription, so the amount you actually paid was less than the list price. The refund is calculated from what left your account, not from the list price. Calculating it the other way would return you more than you ever gave us, which would not make the policy generous — it would make it wrong.

Four visits a year, entered through a single visit

You paid 95,000 ֏ AMD (160,000 less the 65,000 credit)

1 visit done, 3 not performed: 95,000 × 3 ÷ 4 = 71,250 → 71,300 ֏ AMD

Four visits a year, bought outright

You paid 160,000 ֏ AMD

2 visits done, 2 not performed: 160,000 × 2 ÷ 4 = 80,000 ֏ AMD

Six visits a year, entered through an inspection

You paid 180,000 ֏ AMD (200,000 less the 20,000 credit)

1 visit done, 5 not performed: 180,000 × 5 ÷ 6 = 150,000 ֏ AMD

All prices are in Armenian drams (֏ AMD).

Cancelling

Cancellation is done in your account, without telephoning us. The arithmetic above is shown on screen, with your own numbers in it, before you confirm anything. When you confirm, the subscription closes at once and the refund is issued automatically — you do not have to ask for it separately.

Your existing reports stay available to you after cancellation. They are read-only; there are no further visits, and we will not advertise to you on those screens.

If you are unhappy with a report

If a report shows work that was not done properly, tell us and we return and redo the visit at our cost. The window is seven days from the moment the report reaches you, not from the date of the visit. This is a repeat visit rather than a refund. If you would rather cancel, the rule above applies.

One-off visits

An inspection or a single visit can be cancelled for a full refund up to 24 hours before the agreed date. Cancelled later than that, we keep 10,000 ֏ AMD for the trip — the crew and the route are committed to that day — and return the rest. The 10,000 ֏ is shown as its own line on your refund.
