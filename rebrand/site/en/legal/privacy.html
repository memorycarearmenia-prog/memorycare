Privacy policy

We ask for a name and a phone number so we can call you back. If you become a client, we hold the location of your family’s plot, photographs and video of it, and the names and contacts of the people you invite to see them. We do not sell any of it, we do not use it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it by writing to info@memorycare.am.

Who we are

MemoryCare LLC, a company registered in the Republic of Armenia, trading as MemoryCare. Contact for anything in this policy: info@memorycare.am, or Hayk Manukyan on +374 93 154 108.

What we collect, and why

If you only ask for a consultation, we hold your name, your phone number or email address, the cemetery or city you named, your preferred language, anything you chose to write in the note field, and which page you came from. That is the entire form. We do not ask for your address or your family’s names before we have spoken.

If you become a client we additionally hold: your email address, for signing in and for report notifications; the cemetery, sector and plot number of the plot we look after, and its GPS coordinates; photographs and video of the plot and its monuments, made on every visit, and the crew’s written notes; the GPS point recorded by the crew at each visit, with its date and time; your payment record — what you paid, when, and by what method; and the names and contact details of anyone you invite into your Family Circle.

Card numbers never reach us. A card payment is entered on the bank’s own payment page and we never see, hold or store the number.

Who else sees it

  • Our own staff and field crews see the plot, the address, the schedule and the report, to do the work and check it.
  • Our platform developer has technical access to the live system while building and maintaining it.
  • Our customer-relations provider receives consultation requests: name, contact, language, note and source, so that none is lost.
  • Our bank and its payment page receive payment data, in order to take payment.
  • Anyone you invite to your Family Circle sees the reports you give them access to, and anyone you send a report link to sees that one report.

How long we keep it

Your reports remain available to you after your subscription ends. Access to reports about a family member’s grave is not something we switch off when the money stops.

[retention periods for consultation requests that never became clients, and for accounting records, are being set with our lawyer and accountant and will be printed here as numbers]

The name of the person buried in the plot

A report shows the cemetery, the sector and the plot number. The name of the person buried there is not shown unless you switch it on. It is off by default, the setting belongs to the plot, and turning it off again also removes the name from report links that have already been shared.

We do this because a report link travels — into a family group chat, to relatives without accounts — and because part of our audience lives where that kind of data is protected by law.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use photographs of your plot in any marketing, on any channel, without your separate written consent given for that purpose. We do not use your data to make automated decisions about you.

Your choices, and how to use them

Write to info@memorycare.am and ask us to:

  1. send you a copy of what we hold about you;
  2. correct anything that is wrong;
  3. delete your data and close your account;
  4. stop showing the name on the monument, on future and already-shared report links;
  5. remove someone from your Family Circle, which takes effect immediately;
  6. revoke a report link you have shared.

Deleting an account does not delete records we are legally required to keep for accounting.
