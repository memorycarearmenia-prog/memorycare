Cookie policy

This is a separate document from the privacy policy, because what a site stores on your device is a separate question from what a company holds about you.

These pages set no advertising cookies and no analytics cookies. There is no tracking pixel, no advertising network tag, and no third-party script that follows you to another site. That is why you are not asked to accept anything when you arrive.

Two things do store something, and both are strictly necessary. Choosing a language stores that choice on your own device so the site opens in it next time. Signing in to the client portal creates a session cookie that keeps you signed in and ends when you sign out.

When you send the consultation form, what you typed is sent to us together with the page you came from. That is a transfer of data, not a cookie, and it is covered by the privacy policy and by the consent line on the form itself.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser at any time. Blocking the two above means the site forgets your language and the portal cannot keep you signed in; nothing else on these pages changes.

If we ever add measurement or an embedded map, we will ask you first and this page will say what was added and when.
