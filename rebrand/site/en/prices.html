Questions about the price

Do prices differ for clients outside Armenia?

No. One price list, the same for a client in Yerevan and a client in Los Angeles, in Armenian drams. Any figure we show in another currency is approximate and moves with the exchange rate; the amount charged is the dram amount on this page.

Is a second single visit cheaper?

No. A single visit is 65,000 ֏ AMD every time. If you take an annual subscription within 60 days of one, the whole 65,000 ֏ is credited into it, and that visit counts as the first visit of the year.

Does a larger plot cost more?

Up to 16 m² and two monuments, no: the published price is the price. Beyond that there is one formula, the same for everyone: +10,000 ֏ AMD a year for each square metre above 16, and +30,000 ֏ AMD a year for each monument above two. The calculator above shows the arithmetic.

What is credited, and within what window?

The inspection at 20,000 ֏ AMD is credited only when you sign an annual subscription, within 60 days. A single visit at 65,000 ֏ AMD is credited in full into an annual subscription within the same window. One credit only: if you have paid for both, the larger of the two is credited and the inspection remains a paid inspection.

Can I pay in instalments, or season by season?

No. A subscription is paid once, for the year. Paying in parts was considered and decided against, and we would rather say so on this page than at checkout. If the annual amount is not convenient now, start with an inspection or a single visit — what you pay is credited later.

How do I pay, and when is the money taken?

A subscription is paid once, for the year. Today that is a bank transfer against an invoice we send after the conversation. Card payment is being enabled with our bank and we do not promise a date for it. The price is the same either way.
