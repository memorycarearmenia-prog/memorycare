How grave care in Yerevan works

What happens, in what order, and what we do not do. No account is needed to read this.

  1. 01

    You ask

    You send three fields, or write on WhatsApp. We call or write within one business day, Yerevan business hours, 09:00–18:00 (UTC+4). The conversation is about the plot: where it is, what is on it, what condition it is in. Nothing is signed on that call.

  2. 02

    We find the plot

    Yerevan’s cemeteries are not usefully mapped, so this comes first. We locate the plot and record its GPS point; every crew after that goes to those coordinates. If you do not know exactly where it is, this answers it.

  3. 03

    The crew visits

    Before the work: the GPS point, four photographs from four fixed angles, and a walk-round video of 20–40 seconds. Then the work, on the whole plot and every monument. Then the same four angles, and the second video.

  4. 04

    The report reaches you

    Your report arrives within 48 hours: eight photographs, two videos, the GPS point, the date and time, and the crew’s note. It arrives as a link you can forward, and whoever opens it needs no account.

What a full visit includes

  • The whole plot and every monument on it, every time.
  • Steam cleaning of the stone, with a steam generator.
  • Industrial wet-and-dry vacuum extraction.
  • Soft brushes and plastic scrapers only. No wire, ever.
  • pH-neutral products, chosen for the stone under them.
  • Weeding, clearing, levelling, and the rubbish taken away.
  • High pressure for paths and railings, not for monuments.
  • A tripod, so the before and after angles match.

We do not steam a monument that does not need it. Cleaning stone harder than it needs is how stone gets damaged, and on tuf it cannot be undone.

Your plot is looked after by the same assigned crew.

The first visit of a subscription is a full visit like every other, and usually the longest, because a plot nobody has worked on takes longer. It is not a survey: the inspection is the survey, and a separate product.

What we do not do

  • No chlorine and no acid, on any stone, for any reason.
  • No repairs or construction without the municipality’s permission.
  • No work on a neighbouring plot, and no other grave in a photograph.
  • No painting, re-lettering or moving part of a monument without agreeing it first.

The full list, on the limitations page

Winter

Stone cannot be washed at or below +4…+10 °C, or with a frost due within 48 hours: water in the pores freezes and splits the surface from inside, and the damage shows two years later rather than the same day. So we do not promise the date of the winter visit in advance. It runs on the days the weather allows, and we confirm the date with you a few days ahead. If no such day comes all winter, that visit is not lost and it is not replaced with something smaller: it is added to spring, and spring has two visits. Four full visits, whatever the winter does. It is a term of the contract, not an exception to it.
