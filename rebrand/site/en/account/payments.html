How payment works

You can pay by bank transfer today. Card payment is being enabled with our bank and we do not promise a date for it. The price is the same either way: there is no surcharge and no different condition for paying by card.

Transactions made inside Armenia are made in Armenian drams. All our prices are quoted in drams and every charge is made in drams.

The price is the same whichever way you pay.

There is no free trial and no preferential trial period for this service. Nothing renews by itself and nothing is charged automatically.
