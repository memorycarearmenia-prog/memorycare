Questions

Frequently asked

What exactly is included in a visit?

Cleaning and tidying of the grave and the plot, care of flowers and plants (seasonal), minor upkeep — and a photo & video report with GPS and date after every visit.

How do I receive the report?

In your personal account, after each visit: photos and video, with the geotag and date on record. The whole family can be given access.

How do I pay, and in which currency?

Payment is a yearly subscription in Armenian dram (֏). Prices are shown in USD/EUR/RUB for reference only; the currency calculator is informational.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Tell us and we'll make it right — the before/after report is there precisely so expectations and results are clear. Terms and conditions are transparent.
