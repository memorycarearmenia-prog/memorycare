01.1 · The Idea, in Full

Montec belongs to the "old money" tradition of luxury: value carried quietly, legible only to those who know what they are looking at. The logo is small. The leather is the loudest thing in the room, and it never raises its voice. Our customer is the founder, the financier, the lawyer — someone whose success is already established and who wants an object that reflects it without announcing it.

We are not affordable luxury. We are earned luxury — the world's finest materials, made at home in Armenia, offered only to those who ask.

Quiet. Restraint over spectacle. We remove before we add.
Permanent. Made to outlast trends and, ideally, their owner.
Ours. Armenian craft carried with pride, at home and across the diaspora.

“I am not rich enough to buy cheap things.”
The line for the object itself — why the price is the price.
“Craftsmanship and strategy for those who write history.”
The line for the customer — who Montec is made for.

02 · The Old-Money Code
The rules that make it luxury.
01

No logos, loud

The mark is a whisper, never a billboard. Recognition is for insiders, not passers-by.

02

No discounts, ever

The price is the price. A markdown is a confession that the first price was a lie.

03

By application

You are admitted, not sold to. The purchase is an acceptance, and scarcity is real.

04

Numbered batches

Limited, dated, finite. Owning an early piece means something and always will.

05

Material honesty

Full-grain, vegetable-tanned. We show the making. Nothing is corrected or hidden.

06

Made at home

Italian leather, Armenian hands. Local production is not a compromise — it is the point.

03.1 · The Wordmark & The Monogram
MONTEC set in Cormorant Garamond, medium weight, with generous 0.22em letter-spacing. The tracking is the signature. Always upper-case. Never condensed, never italic, never re-drawn.

Two overlapping triangular peaks forming a stylized mountain silhouette — the "Mont" of Montec, made visible. This is the only mark that ever touches the product, embossed tonally — felt more than seen.

03.6 · Don'ts
Six ways to break the mark.
×

Don't stretch

Never distort the proportions. Scale uniformly, always.

×

Don't change tracking or weight

0.22em spacing and medium weight are the wordmark.

×

Don't fill with colour

No ink, no foil, no gradient — blind emboss, tone-on-tone, always.

×

Don't redraw the peaks

Fixed geometry. No rounding, no simplifying.

×

Don't place on clutter

Busy or low-contrast grounds need a solid clearspace pad.

×

Don't default to foil

Gold foil is for hero/cover imagery only, never everyday branding.

04.1 · Primary Palette

A disciplined, three-plus-one palette. Ratio guide — Obsidian 70% · Anthracite/Paper 24% · Brass 6%. Brass is a seasoning, not a sauce.

Obsidian
#0A0A0A
The ground. Backgrounds, the wordmark, the product's soul.
Brass

The single accent. Hardware, rules, emphasis. Use sparingly.

The mid-tone. Panels, dividers, depth without contrast.

05.3 · Armenian & Cyrillic Companions

Cormorant Garamond and Inter are Latin-only. English is the launch language; Armenian and Russian follow. A companion pair is specified now rather than improvised later.

Ա ա Մ մ
Noto Serif Armenian
Pairs with Cormorant Garamond. Free, open-source (SIL OFL), full Armenian Unicode coverage.
Ա ա Ընկերություն
Noto Sans Armenian
Pairs with Inter for body/UI/captions. Same Noto family as the display companion.
А а МОНТЕК
Cyrillic (Russian)
Inter's own set already covers Cyrillic — verify the exact build. If Cormorant lacks Cyrillic, use Noto Serif (Cyrillic) as fallback.

A recommendation to sign off before the Armenian/Russian copy pass begins — not yet used in production.

06.1 · We Say / We Never Say

We speak the way our customer does: fewer words, chosen well. We never hype, never exclaim, never chase.

We say
  • The price is the price.
  • Made at home, from the world's finest leather.
  • Owned by those who ask.
  • It will outlast the trend, and probably you.
  • Quiet is the loudest luxury.
We never say
  • Sale · Discount · Limited-time offer
  • Luxurious!! · Amazing deal · Must-have
  • Buy now · Don't miss out · Hurry
  • Cheap · Affordable · Budget
  • Anything with an exclamation mark.

10.1 · The Pattern

A fine brass hairline grid on obsidian — quiet enough to read as texture, structured enough to nod at a topographic map.

When to use
Packaging interiors, print document inside-covers, digital section dividers — behind a dominant headline or logo.
When to avoid
Never behind hero photography, never at full opacity, never combined with the gold-foil logo.
